## Products

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When will I see results?">
    Results build over time with consistent daily use:

    - **7 days** — Most users notice improved oil control and smoother skin texture.
    - **2–4 weeks** — You should see a visible reduction in active breakouts.
    - **8–12 weeks** — Full results, including clearer, more even skin tone.

    In a clinical study overseen by Gregory W. Chernoff, M.D., F.R.C.S., **95% of participants reported positive results**, many within the first few days.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Neutralyze safe for sensitive skin?">
    Yes. All Neutralyze products are **fragrance-free, sulfate-free, paraben-free, and non-comedogenic** — formulated to minimize irritation. Mandelic acid is also one of the gentlest AHAs available, making it a good fit for sensitive or reactive skin.

    Before applying any Neutralyze product to your full face, **patch-test for 3 days** on a small area (such as your inner forearm or jawline) to make sure your skin tolerates it well.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Neutralyze with retinol, adapalene, or tretinoin?">
    Yes — just stagger your application to avoid over-exfoliation:

    - **Morning:** Apply your Neutralyze product(s).
    - **Evening:** Apply your retinoid (retinol, adapalene, or tretinoin).

    Note that the **Acne Face Wash rinses off** and pairs freely with any retinoid routine. As always, consult your dermatologist whenever you're combining active ingredients.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Neutralyze contain benzoyl peroxide?">
    No. All three Neutralyze products are **completely benzoyl peroxide-free**. You don't need to worry about bleaching your towels, pillowcases, or clothing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Neutralyze safe if I have a nut allergy?">
    Yes. Neutralyze uses **synthesized mandelic acid** — it is not extracted from almonds or any other tree nut. If you have a nut allergy, you can use Neutralyze products safely. If you have additional concerns, please consult your allergist.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Neutralyze while pregnant or breastfeeding?">
    Please **consult your doctor or OB-GYN** before using any acne treatment while pregnant or nursing. This applies to all topical actives, including salicylic acid and mandelic acid, regardless of concentration.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
