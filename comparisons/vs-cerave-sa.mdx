CeraVe SA products and Neutralyze both contain salicylic acid, but they're not built for the same purpose. CeraVe SA is a barrier-repair cleanser designed for general skin smoothing and rough or bumpy texture. Neutralyze is an **acne treatment first** — every formulation decision, from active concentrations to delivery method, is made with moderate-to-severe acne in mind. If you're comparing the two, the distinction matters.

## CeraVe SA: General Exfoliating Skincare

CeraVe SA products lead with skin-barrier ingredients — ceramides, hyaluronic acid, niacinamide — and include salicylic acid at a modest concentration (typically around 0.5%) to provide gentle exfoliation. That concentration is well-suited to mild roughness, keratosis pilaris, or general skin smoothing, and the barrier-focused formula makes it a popular option for everyday skincare routines.

What CeraVe SA is not specifically formulated to do is target persistent moderate-to-severe acne. At 0.5% salicylic acid with no secondary acid, it doesn't deliver the therapeutic concentration or dual-acid action that acne treatment requires.

## Neutralyze: Acne Treatment Formulated for Therapeutic Results

Neutralyze products use **2% salicylic acid** — the maximum strength available over the counter — paired with **1% mandelic acid**. The salicylic acid penetrates into pores to clear the congestion behind blackheads and breakouts; the mandelic acid works at the surface to fade post-acne dark spots and promote even cell turnover. The Renewal Complex also uses **time-released delivery** to maintain consistent active levels in your skin throughout the day rather than delivering one burst at application.

These choices are intentional: they're the difference between a product that generally supports skin health and one that's specifically calibrated to treat acne.

## Which One Is Right for You?

If your main concern is mild roughness or general skin texture and you love CeraVe's barrier-support approach, that product may be exactly what you need. But if you're dealing with persistent breakouts — blackheads, whiteheads, papules, or cysts — and you need a treatment-strength formula, Neutralyze is designed for that use case. The therapeutic difference between 0.5% and 2% salicylic acid, plus the addition of mandelic acid, is meaningful when acne is the goal.
