Most acne treatments pick one acid and call it a day. Neutralyze uses two — **2% salicylic acid and 1% mandelic acid** — because each one targets a different layer of the problem. Salicylic acid works deep inside your pores while mandelic acid renews the surface and fades the marks breakouts leave behind. Together, they cover the full cycle of acne in a single step, which is why you'll find both in every Neutralyze Face Wash, Exfoliating Pad, and Renewal Complex.

## What Salicylic Acid Does

Salicylic acid is a **beta hydroxy acid (BHA)**, and its defining trait is that it's oil-soluble. That means it doesn't just sit on the surface of your skin — it actually travels down into your pores, where oil and dead skin cells cause the congestion behind blackheads, whiteheads, and inflamed breakouts.

Once inside the pore, salicylic acid:

- **Dissolves the plug** of oil and dead skin that clogs the follicle
- **Reduces inflammation**, which is why it helps calm red, swollen blemishes faster
- **Controls excess oil** at the source, making future breakouts less likely

At 2% — the maximum strength available over the counter — salicylic acid in Neutralyze delivers the most effective OTC dose for moderate to severe acne.

## What Mandelic Acid Does

Mandelic acid is an **alpha hydroxy acid (AHA)**. Unlike salicylic acid, it's water-soluble, so it works at the skin's surface rather than inside the pore. Its job is exfoliation: gently loosening dead skin cells so your skin can shed them evenly instead of letting them pile up and dull your complexion.

What makes mandelic acid stand out among AHAs is its large molecular size. Because it absorbs slowly, it causes less irritation than glycolic acid — making it the right choice for daily use on acne-prone and sensitive skin. It's particularly effective at:

- **Fading post-acne dark spots** (post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation)
- **Smoothing uneven texture** left behind after breakouts heal
- **Supporting cell turnover** so fresh, clearer skin comes through faster

## Why the Combination Works Better

Salicylic acid clears the pore from the inside out. Mandelic acid renews the surface from the outside in. Used alone, salicylic acid can clear breakouts but doesn't address the discoloration they leave. Mandelic acid alone can improve tone and texture, but it can't penetrate the pore to stop new breakouts at the source.

Together, they treat the active breakout *and* the aftermath simultaneously — so you're not just treating acne, you're working toward clearer, more even-toned skin at the same time.

## Salicylic Acid vs. Mandelic Acid: Side-by-Side

| | Salicylic Acid | Mandelic Acid |
|---|---|---|
| **Type** | Beta hydroxy acid (BHA) | Alpha hydroxy acid (AHA) |
| **Solubility** | Oil-soluble | Water-soluble |
| **Primary action** | Unclogs pores, dissolves oil plugs, reduces inflammation | Exfoliates surface, fades dark spots, evens skin tone |
| **Best for** | Blackheads, whiteheads, active breakouts, oily skin | Post-acne marks, uneven texture, sensitive or darker skin tones |
| **Gentleness** | Moderate | One of the gentlest AHAs (large molecule, slow absorption) |
