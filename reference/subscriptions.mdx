The Neutralyze Subscribe & Save program is the easiest way to keep your skincare routine on track without ever running out of product. When you subscribe, you automatically save **15% on every order**, your supply renews on your chosen schedule, and you're free to pause, skip, or cancel at any time — no penalties, no minimum commitment.

---

## Subscription Benefits

| Benefit | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Discount** | 15% off every order, automatically applied |
| **Flexibility** | Pause, skip, or cancel anytime |
| **Cancellation fee** | None — cancel at any time, for any reason |
| **Guarantee coverage** | Subscription orders are covered by the 60-day money-back guarantee |

---

## How to Sign Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a product to your cart">
    Visit [neutralyze.com](https://www.neutralyze.com) and add your chosen Neutralyze product to your cart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select 'Subscribe & Save'">
    On the product page or in your cart, choose the **Subscribe & Save** option instead of "One-Time Purchase." Your 15% discount will be reflected immediately in the price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete checkout">
    Finish your checkout as normal. Your subscription starts automatically, and future orders will ship on your selected renewal schedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Managing Your Subscription

You have full control over your subscription at any time:

- **Online:** Log in to your account at [neutralyze.com](https://www.neutralyze.com) to view your subscription, change your renewal date, skip a shipment, or update payment and shipping details.
- **By email:** Contact [customerservice@neutralyze.com](mailto:customerservice@neutralyze.com) and our team will handle any changes for you.
- **By phone:** Call toll-free **855-460-ACNE**.

---

## How to Cancel

You can cancel your subscription **at any time with no cancellation fee**. To avoid being charged for an upcoming renewal, cancel before the next scheduled billing date. Orders that have already been processed will ship as normal and remain covered by the 60-day guarantee.

To cancel, contact us via:
- **Email:** [customerservice@neutralyze.com](mailto:customerservice@neutralyze.com)
- **Phone:** 855-460-ACNE

---

<Tip>
  **Building up a supply?** Before you cancel, consider **pausing or skipping**
  your next shipment instead. This keeps your discount and subscription status
  active so you can resume whenever you're ready — without having to sign up
  again.
</Tip>
