Getting Neutralyze into your hands quickly is a priority. Orders are processed fast — most ship within 24–48 hours of purchase — and many are fulfilled through Amazon's fulfillment network for added speed and reliability. Here's everything you need to know about shipping before you order.

---

## Shipping at a Glance

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Free shipping threshold** | Orders over $30 ship free (standard) |
| **Order processing time** | Within 24–48 hours of purchase |
| **Estimated delivery** | 4–6 business days |
| **Shipping destination** | United States only |

<Info>
  **Free Shipping:** Standard shipping is free on all US orders totaling **$30
  or more**. Orders under $30 may be subject to a standard shipping fee at
  checkout.
</Info>

---

## Delivery Details

- **Processing:** Your order is prepared and dispatched within **24–48 hours** of purchase on business days.
- **Transit time:** After your order ships, expect delivery in **4–6 business days** via standard shipping carriers.
- **Fulfillment:** Many Neutralyze orders are fulfilled through Amazon's fulfillment network, which means fast, trackable delivery straight to your door.

---

## International Shipping

At this time, Neutralyze ships to **US addresses only**. International shipping is not currently available. We hope to expand in the future — check back for updates.

---

<Note>
  **Subscription orders** qualify for free standard shipping on the same terms
  as one-time orders. If your subscription order totals $30 or more, shipping is
  on us. Learn more about [subscriptions](/reference/subscriptions).
</Note>
