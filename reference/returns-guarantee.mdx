We stand behind every Neutralyze product, unconditionally. If you're not completely satisfied with your results within 60 days of purchase, you get your money back — no questions asked, no return shipment required. That's our promise to you.

---

## The 60-Day Guarantee

Here's exactly what the Neutralyze money-back guarantee covers:

| Term | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Guarantee window** | 60 days from date of purchase |
| **Refund amount** | 100% of the product purchase price, less original shipping charges |
| **Return required?** | No — keep or discard the product |
| **Limit per customer** | One item per customer; for multiple purchases, the most recent transaction is refunded |

<Note>
  **No return needed.** You do not have to ship anything back to receive your
  refund. Simply contact our customer service team, and we'll process your
  refund directly. There are no hoops to jump through.
</Note>

## Damaged or Defective Orders

If your order arrives damaged or leaking, we'll replace it at no cost to you. Contact customer service as soon as possible with your order details, and we'll get a replacement on the way.

## How to Request a Refund

Requesting a refund is straightforward:

1. **Email** [customerservice@neutralyze.com](mailto:customerservice@neutralyze.com) with your order number and a brief note about your experience.
2. **Alternatively**, call us toll-free at **855-460-ACNE** or use the live chat at [neutralyze.com](https://www.neutralyze.com).
3. Our team will confirm your refund and process it promptly — no return label, no restocking fee.
