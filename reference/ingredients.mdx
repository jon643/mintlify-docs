Neutralyze is built on the belief that clear skin should never come at the cost of your health or trust. Every formula is fragrance-free, paraben-free, non-comedogenic, and cruelty-free — with each active ingredient chosen for a specific, evidence-backed role in treating acne. Below you'll find the full INCI (International Nomenclature of Cosmetic Ingredients) lists for all three Neutralyze products, exactly as they appear on product packaging.

## Important Notes

<Info>
  **Nut Allergy Safety:** The mandelic acid used in all Neutralyze products is
  **synthetically synthesized** — it is not extracted from almonds or any other
  tree nut. If you have a nut allergy, you can use Neutralyze products with
  confidence. As always, if you have concerns about a specific sensitivity,
  consult your dermatologist before use.
</Info>

### Formulation Standards

All three Neutralyze products are:

- **Non-comedogenic** — formulated to avoid clogging pores
- **Fragrance-free** — no added perfumes or masking fragrances
- **Paraben-free** — no parabens used as preservatives
- **Cruelty-free** — never tested on animals

### Manufacturing & Testing

Neutralyze products are manufactured in the United States in an **FDA-registered, cGMP-certified facility**. Every product undergoes:

| Test | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| RIPT skin-sensitivity testing | Confirms the formula does not cause sensitization reactions |
| Stability testing | Verifies ingredient integrity and shelf life |
| Regulatory OTC testing | Ensures compliance with FDA OTC monograph requirements |
