Acne does not stop at the jawline. Body acne — especially on the back (bacne), chest, and shoulders — is extremely common and often more stubborn than facial acne because body skin is thicker, pores are larger, and the area tends to stay covered under clothing that traps heat and sweat. The good news: all three Neutralyze products are approved for use on the face, chest, back, and body, and the format of each product makes them particularly well suited to the unique challenges of treating skin below the neck.

## Which Products Work Best for Body Acne

<Accordion title="Exfoliating Pads — Best for On-the-Go and Post-Workout Treatment">
  The Exfoliating Pads are your workhorse for body acne. The pre-saturated pad format means no measuring, no pouring, and no mess — you can grab a pad, wipe across your chest, back, or shoulders, and leave it to absorb without rinsing. This no-rinse convenience is especially powerful after workouts, when sweat, friction from tight clothing, and bacteria from sports equipment create the perfect storm for clogged pores. Keep a few pads in your gym bag so treatment happens immediately after exercise, not hours later.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Acne Face Wash — Best for In-Shower Cleansing">
  The Face Wash doubles as a body wash for acne-prone zones. Use it in the shower on your chest, back, and shoulders just as you would on your face — massage it in, let it sit for a few seconds, then rinse. This step clears sweat, oil, and surface debris before you apply the leave-on treatments.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Renewal Complex — Best for Larger Surface Areas">
  After toweling off, apply the Renewal Complex to acne-prone body areas for continued treatment and lightweight moisture. The non-greasy formula absorbs cleanly on body skin and covers large areas like the full back or chest without feeling heavy under clothing.
</Accordion>

## Tips for Hard-to-Reach Back Acne

The back is the trickiest area to treat consistently — and consistency is everything with acne. Try these approaches:

- **Use an Exfoliating Pad with an extended-reach tool.** Wrap or attach a pad to a long-handled bath brush or acne applicator tool so you can reach your mid- and lower-back without straining.
- **Ask for help when you can.** For the Renewal Complex in particular, having a partner apply it to the full back once or twice a week makes full coverage far easier.
- **Focus on the areas you can reach well.** Treating the upper back, shoulders, and chest thoroughly and consistently is more effective than inconsistently reaching everywhere.

## Post-Workout Routine

Sweat and friction are two of the biggest triggers for body breakouts. A fast, simple post-workout protocol makes a measurable difference:

1. Change out of sweaty clothing as quickly as possible — sitting in damp workout gear keeps pores blocked and bacteria active.
2. If you can shower, use the Face Wash on affected areas before any other treatment.
3. If you cannot shower right away, use an Exfoliating Pad on the chest, back, and shoulders to neutralize sweat residue and start clearing pores immediately.
4. Once you do shower, follow up with the Renewal Complex on dry skin.

<Tip>
  Always shower first and apply Exfoliating Pads to completely dry skin whenever possible. Wet or damp skin dilutes the active solution on the pad and allows it to spread unpredictably, which can lead to uneven treatment or unnecessary irritation in sensitive areas. Pat dry thoroughly, wait 60 seconds, then swipe the pad.
</Tip>

## What to Expect with Body Acne

Body skin is structurally thicker than facial skin, and the dermis on your back and chest has a higher density of sebaceous glands. This means body acne typically takes a few additional weeks to respond compared to facial acne. Give the routine at least **10–14 weeks** of consistent use before evaluating results. Pair your Neutralyze routine with other practical habits: use clean towels after every shower, wash pillowcases and sheets regularly, and avoid synthetic fabrics against acne-prone skin when possible.
