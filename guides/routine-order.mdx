Getting the sequence right is just as important as using the right products. When you apply Neutralyze in the correct order — lightest to most active — each step preps your skin for the next, actives absorb without interference, and you avoid accidentally diluting or blocking key ingredients. Skipping steps or reversing the order can reduce effectiveness and increase the chance of irritation. Follow the steps below every morning and evening for best results.

## Daily Routine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cleanse — Acne Face Wash">
    Wet your skin, massage in the [Face Wash](/products/face-wash) using gentle circular motions, rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water, and pat dry with a clean towel. This removes excess oil, sweat, and surface debris so that your treatment products can penetrate unobstructed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Treat — Exfoliating Pads">
    Unfold one [Exfoliating Pad](/products/exfoliating-pads) and wipe it across the affected area in smooth, even strokes. Do not rinse — the salicylic and mandelic acid solution needs to stay on your skin to work. If you are just starting out, use pads once daily and build up gradually as your skin adjusts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Moisturize & Treat — Renewal Complex">
    Once your skin feels dry to the touch (about 30–60 seconds after the pad step), apply a thin, even layer of the [Renewal Complex](/products/renewal-complex). This step simultaneously moisturizes and delivers a second layer of active treatment, supporting cell turnover overnight or throughout the day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Protect — SPF 30+ (AM Only)">
    Every morning, finish with a broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher sunscreen. Salicylic acid and mandelic acid both increase your skin's sensitivity to UV radiation, so sun protection is non-negotiable — especially if you are working to fade dark spots. Apply sunscreen after the Renewal Complex has fully absorbed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Makeup (AM Only — Optional)">
    If you wear makeup, wait 3–5 minutes after applying the Renewal Complex before reaching for foundation or concealer. This brief pause lets the complex absorb fully so your makeup goes on smoothly and does not disrupt the active layer underneath.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pairing Neutralyze with Other Actives

You can layer Neutralyze with many popular skincare actives — timing and sequencing just require a little attention.

<Accordion title="Retinoids (retinol, adapalene, tretinoin)">
  Use Neutralyze in the morning and your retinoid at night, or alternate nights if your skin is sensitive to both. The Face Wash rinses off completely and pairs freely with any retinoid routine — there is no conflict at the cleansing step.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Hyaluronic acid and niacinamide">
  Both of these hydrating and barrier-supporting ingredients play well with Neutralyze. Apply them after all three Neutralyze steps have dried, layering over the Renewal Complex for added hydration. Niacinamide also supports fading of dark spots and is an especially good complement.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Vitamin C (L-ascorbic acid)">
  Strong vitamin C serums can conflict with the low-pH environment of exfoliating acids. Use vitamin C at the opposite time of day — for example, Neutralyze in the evening and vitamin C in the morning (or vice versa).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Benzoyl peroxide">
  Avoid layering benzoyl peroxide at the same time of day as Neutralyze exfoliating products — the combination can be overly drying and irritating. If you want to use both, separate them into morning and evening slots, and consult a dermatologist for personalized guidance.
</Accordion>

---

<Warning>
  Salicylic acid and mandelic acid increase your skin's sensitivity to UV radiation. Skipping SPF during the day while using Neutralyze can lead to faster darkening of post-acne marks and increased risk of sun damage. Apply a broad-spectrum SPF 30+ every morning, rain or shine.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Every Neutralyze product — the [Face Wash](/products/face-wash), [Exfoliating Pads](/products/exfoliating-pads), and [Renewal Complex](/products/renewal-complex) — contributes to exfoliation. If you use all three together alongside additional actives like a retinoid, watch closely for signs of over-exfoliation: persistent dryness, tightness, flaking, or irritation. If those appear, scale back the frequency of the Exfoliating Pads first before adjusting other steps.
</Note>
