If your skin reacts easily to new products, you are in the right place. Neutralyze is built around **mandelic acid**, widely recognized as one of the gentlest alpha-hydroxy acids available. Unlike glycolic acid — the most commonly used AHA — mandelic acid has a larger molecular structure that penetrates the skin more slowly and more evenly, delivering effective exfoliation without the sharp sting and redness that glycolic formulas can cause on reactive skin. All Neutralyze products are fragrance-free, sulfate-free, and paraben-free, removing three of the most common sources of sensitivity reactions before you even open the bottle.

## Why Mandelic Acid Is Gentler Than Glycolic Acid

The difference comes down to molecular size. Glycolic acid is the smallest AHA, which means it penetrates rapidly and deeply — effective, but often too aggressive for sensitive or darker skin tones. Mandelic acid's larger molecule moves into the skin more gradually, giving cells time to adjust and reducing the likelihood of a sharp inflammatory response. This makes it a trusted choice for sensitive skin types, rosacea-prone skin (in remission), and skin that has struggled with other exfoliating acids in the past.

## How to Patch Test (3-Day Protocol)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Day 1 — Choose Your Test Area">
    Apply a small amount of the product to an inconspicuous but representative area — behind the ear or along the jawline works well. Use it once and observe over the next 24 hours. Look for prolonged redness, swelling, itching, or a rash (as opposed to a brief mild tingle, which is normal for a low-pH exfoliant).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 2 — Repeat and Observe">
    Apply again to the same area. Note whether any reaction from Day 1 has resolved or is worsening. If worsening, stop testing that product and try the gentlest option (the Face Wash) first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 3 — Confirm and Proceed">
    Apply a third time. If you have had no significant reaction over three days, you can incorporate the product into your regular routine with confidence. Wait at least a week before patch testing the next product.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Starting Slowly — Frequency Ramp-Up

Even after a successful patch test, easing into the full routine gives your skin time to build tolerance.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with the Face Wash Alone">
    The Face Wash is the gentlest entry point because it rinses off — active contact time is brief. Use it morning and evening for at least one week before introducing any leave-on product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add One Leave-On Product in the Evening">
    Introduce either the Exfoliating Pads or the Renewal Complex once daily in the evening. Evening use is ideal because it reduces daytime UV exposure during the adjustment period. Wait one week before adding the second leave-on product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build to Twice Daily as Tolerated">
    Once your skin is comfortable with all three products in the evening, you can begin using the routine in the morning as well. Increase frequency gradually rather than all at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If Irritation Occurs

<Accordion title="Mild dryness or tightness">
  Reduce the Exfoliating Pads to every other day or every two days. Continue the Face Wash and Renewal Complex as usual — the complex provides moisture that can help buffer dryness. Make sure you are following Neutralyze with a broad-spectrum SPF 30+ during the day, as dryness worsens with UV exposure.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Persistent stinging, redness, or flaking">
  Pull back to using only the Face Wash for a few days while your skin recovers. Once the irritation fully resolves, reintroduce the Renewal Complex first (it tends to be gentler than the pads for sensitive skin), and skip the Exfoliating Pads for another week before re-adding them every other day.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Rash, swelling, or breakout in new areas">
  Discontinue use and contact us at [customerservice@neutralyze.com](mailto:customerservice@neutralyze.com). All products are covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee. If you have active rosacea or eczema, please consult your dermatologist before resuming use.
</Accordion>

<Note>
  A brief, mild tingle during the first few applications of the Exfoliating Pads or Renewal Complex is completely normal — it reflects the low-pH environment that makes the actives work. What you want to watch for is tingle that persists beyond a few minutes, or any burning, swelling, or itching that gets worse over repeated use. If you have active rosacea or eczema, consult your dermatologist before starting.
