Teen acne is one of the most common skin concerns, and it deserves a straightforward, no-fuss solution. Hormonal changes during adolescence increase oil production, which clogs pores and feeds acne-causing bacteria — so the best approach is a consistent routine that keeps pores clear without stripping the skin's barrier. Neutralyze is formulated for both teens and adults: fragrance-free, non-comedogenic, and built around salicylic and mandelic acid instead of harsh ingredients like benzoyl peroxide that can bleach towels, pillowcases, and team uniforms.

## Why Neutralyze Works for Teen Skin

Teenage skin is often reactive, and layering too many products at once is a recipe for irritation and frustration. Neutralyze keeps things simple — three products, one consistent routine — and the gentle mandelic acid base means the formula is effective without being unnecessarily aggressive on skin that may still be finding its equilibrium. The no-benzoyl-peroxide formulation is also a practical win: no more bleached pillowcases or ruined jersey collars.

Neutralyze products are also non-comedogenic, so they will not clog pores, and they are fragrance-free, which reduces the risk of sensitivity reactions in skin that is already dealing with inflammation.

## Recommended Starter Routine for Teens

Start with one product at a time — adding everything at once makes it impossible to know what is (or is not) working.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Week 1 — Start with the Face Wash">
    Use the Acne Face Wash morning and night. This alone starts clearing pore congestion and builds the core habit. Wet your face, massage in the wash, rinse thoroughly, and pat dry. Keep it consistent for a full week before adding anything new.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Week 2 — Add the Exfoliating Pads">
    Once your skin has adjusted to the Face Wash, add one Exfoliating Pad once daily after washing — evening is a great time to start. Swipe it across breakout-prone areas and leave it on; do not rinse. If you play sports, using a pad soon after practice is especially helpful: sweat and friction from equipment or tight clothing are major triggers for teen breakouts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Week 3 and Beyond — Add the Renewal Complex">
    Introduce the Renewal Complex as your moisturizer after the Exfoliating Pad has dried. Apply a thin, even layer. This step keeps the treatment working between washes and replaces any separate moisturizer you might have been using.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Before starting, do a quick patch test: apply each new product to a small area (like the side of your jaw) for 3 days before using it on your whole face. This is a good habit for any new skincare product.
</Note>

## What to Expect

Most teens see meaningful improvement within **8–12 weeks** of consistent use. The first few weeks can sometimes bring an increase in breakouts as the actives accelerate the clearing of already-congested pores — this is called purging, and it is a normal, temporary response. Stay consistent, resist the urge to over-treat, and give the routine time to work.

Results are only as good as the routine that delivers them. Skipping days, especially during a breakout, slows progress. Once-daily use at a minimum is the floor; twice daily gets you there faster.

<Info>
  Neutralyze is safe and effective for teens 12 and older. For teens who play sports regularly, the Exfoliating Pads are especially useful post-workout for the back, chest, and shoulders — areas where equipment friction commonly triggers breakouts. See the [Body Acne guide](/guides/best-for-body-acne) for more tips.

<Note>
  If your acne is severe, widespread, or cystic (deep, painful nodules under the skin), please consult a board-certified dermatologist before or in addition to starting an OTC routine. Cystic acne often requires prescription treatment, and a dermatologist can also help rule out underlying hormonal factors. For children under 12, consult a pediatrician or dermatologist before starting any acne treatment.
</Note>
