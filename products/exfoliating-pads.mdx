## Directions

<Warning>
  **Patch test first.** If you're new to chemical exfoliants, apply a pad sparingly to one or two small affected areas for the first 3 days. Wait to see how your skin responds before applying more widely.
</Warning>

1. **Wipe** a pad over the affected area using gentle, even strokes.
2. **Do not rinse.** Let the solution absorb into your skin.
3. **Start once daily** and increase to two or three times daily as your skin adjusts.
4. **Reduce frequency** to once a day or every other day if you experience bothersome dryness, peeling, or irritation.

<Tip>
  For body breakouts on your chest or back, use the textured side of the pad for a deeper cleanse, then flip to the smooth side to apply the treatment solution.
</Tip>
