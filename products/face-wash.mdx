## Directions

1. **Wet** your face or the affected area with warm water.
2. **Massage** a desired amount gently into your skin using circular motions for 30–60 seconds.
3. **Rinse thoroughly** with warm water until all product is removed.
4. **Pat dry** — do not rub.
5. **Use morning and night** as the first step in your routine before any leave-on actives.

<Note>
  Because the Face Wash fully rinses off, it does **not** add to the cumulative acid load of your routine. It pairs freely with leave-on actives like retinoids, prescription topicals, and other PM treatments — you don't need to worry about overloading your skin.
</Note>

<Tip>
  **Body acne?** The Face Wash works just as well on your chest and back as it does on your face. Use it in the shower on any breakout-prone areas — lather it on, let it sit for 30 seconds, then rinse.
</Tip>
