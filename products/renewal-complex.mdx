The Neutralyze Renewal Complex is not your average acne moisturizer — it's a time-released, all-in-one treatment cream that keeps working long after you apply it. Its dual-acid formula of 2% salicylic acid (BHA) and 1% mandelic acid (AHA) is locked into a lightweight, fast-absorbing base that continuously delivers actives to your skin throughout the day and overnight. You get acne treatment, blemish control, and lasting hydration in a single step, replacing the need for a separate moisturizer in your routine.

## Directions

1. **Cleanse first.** Use the [Neutralyze Face Wash](/products/face-wash) or complete the [Exfoliating Pads](/products/exfoliating-pads) step, then wait until your skin is fully dry.
2. **Apply a thin, even layer** to the entire affected area — face, or wherever you experience breakouts.
3. **Do not rinse.** The formula is designed to stay on your skin.
4. **Use morning and night** for continuous coverage of active delivery.
5. **Allow 3–5 minutes to absorb** before applying SPF, foundation, or other makeup.
6. **Start with one application daily** and increase as tolerated; reduce frequency if dryness or irritation occurs.

<Note>
  The Renewal Complex uses a time-release delivery system that continuously disperses salicylic acid and mandelic acid into your skin for hours after application — so even a single morning or evening layer keeps working all day or overnight without requiring extra applications.
</Note>

<Tip>
  The Renewal Complex is not a retinoid, so you can pair it with a prescription or OTC retinoid at night. Many users apply Renewal Complex in the morning and their retinoid in the evening for comprehensive treatment.
</Tip>
